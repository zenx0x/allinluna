# Research Routes

Keep a research terrain map separate from decisions and execution.

1. Inventory candidate routes and source-backed evidence.
2. Keep observations, interpretations, Claims, Evidence, contradictions, and unknowns distinct.
3. Compare assumptions, mature-method comparators, costs, failure regimes, and falsifiable probes.
4. Choose only a reversible next probe when the user authorizes exploration.
5. State that a terrain map is not experiment authorization, implementation, HumanDecision, or canonical-state promotion.

When a route is authorized for product work, hand off its bounded decision and evidence package to `$allinluna-plan`; do not silently turn research notes into implementation tasks.
