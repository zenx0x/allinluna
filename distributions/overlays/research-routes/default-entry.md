Start with `$research-routes` to build a route-neutral `research-pack/v1` terrain map, separate Claims from Evidence, preserve every polarity and contradiction, compare competing approaches with mature-method limits, and choose a reversible next probe. Record `FailureRecord`, `RewindProposal`, `Lesson`, `ReopenedProblem`, and canonical downgrade without deleting history. Route authorization must name a confirmed HumanDecision, and canonical promotion needs its own explicit decision.
